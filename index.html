Puter account check ho raha hai…
Puter account bilkul free hai — sirf email se banta hai, koi bank card nahi chahiye. Har free account ko har month kuch free AI allowance milta hai; sawal usi allowance se bante hain, is website ko kuch pay nahi karna parta. Ek dafa sign in kar lein to browser yaad rakhta hai — har dafa sign in nahi karna parta.
Ye mazboot AI hai (GPT/Claude class) jo photo parh kar sawal bana kar deta hai — koi API key nahi chahiye, bas upar wala ek-dafa FREE Puter sign in chahiye. Jawab aam taur par seconds se ek minute mein aa jata hai. Behtar result ke liye: ek safha = ek photo, qareeb se, saaf roshni mein.
Slow tareeqa ek free CPU server (Qwen2.5-VL) use karta hai — sign in nahi chahiye, lekin asal test mein ye 10–12 minute mein bhi time out ho gaya tha. Upar wala Puter wala tareeqa hi behtar hai; ye sirf backup hai.
